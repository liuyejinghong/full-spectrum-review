## Why case-07's assertion changed

The original case demanded a verbatim excerpt and explicit narration of a
stale source comment even for a self-contained P2 finding. The core required
excerpts for P0/P1 and load-bearing cross-unit evidence, not every P2. Both
independent auditors cited the real mechanism and did not inherit the stale
comment. Failing solely because they did not repeat that unused comment tested
extra narration rather than evidence correctness.

An attempted broadening of the protocol to require that narration was withdrawn.
The final judge instead checks that the cited source actually contains the
inline await and serial send, and rejects a stale/non-covering citation. P0/P1
excerpt requirements remain unchanged. This evaluator correction is explicit;
the original failure is not concealed or described as an auditor improvement.

The second fresh probe independently found the same field-loss and scheduling/
partial-fill mechanisms, with correct source locations. It was a diagnostic
probe, not another complete seven-case run. Its case-01 priority differed from
the first auditor and its P1 excerpt was missing, so its raw report was not used
as the accepted canonical report. That variation remains an output-quality
limitation rather than grounds to add another protocol guard.
